The Reactive Trap

Without predictive support, churn is often discovered only after a customer has already left. In our dataset, 883 of 1,000 customers churned, showing why earlier risk identification matters.

  • close Churn is recognized too late for proactive action
  • close Customer risk patterns are difficult to judge consistently by hand
  • close Retention decisions can become reactive instead of data-driven

The ChurnGuard Pivot

ChurnGuard turns structured customer information into a churn-risk prediction using the tuned K-Nearest Neighbours model. The result is stored in a cloud-hosted PostgreSQL database and presented as a probability and risk level to support proactive retention decisions.

Model Inputs

Precision Engineering
for Customer Loyalty

The prediction pipeline uses eight customer features: age, gender, tenure, monthly charges, total charges, tech support, contract type, and internet service. These inputs are cleaned, encoded, standardized, and passed into the tuned KNN model to estimate churn probability.

tune

Structured Preprocessing

Categorical fields are encoded and numerical fields are standardized before prediction.

hub

Tuned KNN Prediction

The final model uses k=11, Manhattan distance, and distance weighting to generate a churn probability and risk level.
